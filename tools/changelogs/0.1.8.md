## 0.1.8

- New: "update available" hint. Players tell each other which version they run (guild, group); if someone has a newer one, a short message appears once
- The unread counter now also notices when Blizzard's hotfix article gets new hotfixes (it used to count only brand-new entries)
- The hotfix article is shown day by day: every day has its own heading, newest first, so older hotfixes are no longer mixed in
- The date is no longer repeated in every sub-heading
- New short summaries: announcement of November 8 and the hotfixes up to November 10
